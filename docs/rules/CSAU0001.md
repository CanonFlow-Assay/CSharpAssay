# CSAU0001 — Extensible configured hierarchy

Status: Prototype
Category: Union
Certainty: deterministic candidate
Disposition: block only after admission and only within qualified scope

## Mechanism

A configured closed base type is inspected for externally callable instance constructors.

## False-positive boundary

Only types named in csassay_closed_types or strict policy are treated as closed.

- Violation specimen: `specimens/Compat.Bad/CSAU0001/`

## Suppression

A compiler pragma or `SuppressMessage` is not authorization. Suppression must
match a reviewed policy grant with a finding fingerprint, owner, reason, and
expiry; otherwise CSAP0001 remains evidence.

## Fix safety

No automatic fix. Make construction closed and keep cases under the declared ownership boundary.
