# CSAA0002 — Blocking async flow

Status: Prototype
Category: Async
Certainty: deterministic candidate
Disposition: block only after admission and only within qualified scope

## Mechanism

Task-like Result property references and Wait invocations are resolved by metadata identity inside async methods.

## False-positive boundary

Synchronous methods are outside this restricted subset even when blocking may still be undesirable.

- Compliant specimen: `specimens/Compat.Good/CSAA0002/`

## Suppression

A compiler pragma or `SuppressMessage` is not authorization. Suppression must
match a reviewed policy grant with a finding fingerprint, owner, reason, and
expiry; otherwise CSAP0001 remains evidence.

## Fix safety

No automatic fix. Await the operation and propagate cancellation through the boundary.
