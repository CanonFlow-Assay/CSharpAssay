# CSAA0001 — Async void

Status: Prototype
Category: Async
Certainty: deterministic candidate
Disposition: block only after admission and only within qualified scope

## Mechanism

Method symbols identify async void methods; recognized two-parameter event handlers are excluded.

## False-positive boundary

Event-handler recognition is deliberately narrow and does not authorize general fire-and-forget work.

## Suppression

A compiler pragma or `SuppressMessage` is not authorization. Suppression must
match a reviewed policy grant with a finding fingerprint, owner, reason, and
expiry; otherwise CSAP0001 remains evidence.

## Fix safety

No automatic fix. Return Task and make ownership of cancellation and failure explicit.
