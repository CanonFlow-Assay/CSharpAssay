# CSAU0004 — Native union discard

Status: Prototype
Category: Union
Certainty: deterministic candidate
Disposition: block only after admission and only within qualified scope

## Mechanism

The pinned SDK adapter identifies native union symbols. Switch inspection reports
discard/default, `var`, `object`, empty untyped property patterns and `not null`.
Parentheses and `or`/`and` combinations preserve this bounded classification.
Guards do not exempt a catch-all shape: future cases can also satisfy the guard.

## False-positive boundary

The .NET 11 compiler exposes native unions through `ITypeSymbol.IsUnion`. Both
switch expressions and statements are covered. Explicit case types, including
typed property patterns and unions of explicit case patterns, are accepted.
Arbitrary property constraints, interface coverage and Boolean guard reasoning
are outside this detector. The compiler retains exhaustiveness authority;
CSAU0004 remains Prototype and cannot become a required admitted blocker.

## Suppression

A compiler pragma or `SuppressMessage` is not authorization. Suppression must
match a reviewed policy grant with a finding fingerprint, owner, reason, and
expiry; otherwise CSAP0001 remains evidence.

## Fix safety

No automatic fix while the native representation is unqualified.
