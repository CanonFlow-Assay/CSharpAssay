# CSAU0004 — Native union discard

Status: Prototype
Category: Union
Certainty: deterministic candidate
Disposition: block only after admission and only within qualified scope

## Mechanism

The qualified SDK adapter identifies native union symbols, then semantic switch inspection reports discard/default arms.

## False-positive boundary

This rule is unavailable until the pinned compiler exposes a qualified native-union symbol surface.

- Compliant specimen: `specimens/Compat.Good/CSAU0004/`

## Suppression

A compiler pragma or `SuppressMessage` is not authorization. Suppression must
match a reviewed policy grant with a finding fingerprint, owner, reason, and
expiry; otherwise CSAP0001 remains evidence.

## Fix safety

No automatic fix while the native representation is unqualified.
