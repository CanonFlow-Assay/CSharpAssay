# CSAP0001 — Unauthorized suppression

Status: Prototype
Category: Policy
Certainty: deterministic candidate
Disposition: block only after admission and only within qualified scope

## Mechanism

Semantic inspection of pragma and SuppressMessage targets whose diagnostic ID belongs to CSharpAssay.

## False-positive boundary

Compiler and non-CSharpAssay suppressions are outside this rule.

## Evidence

- Compliant specimen: `specimens/Compat.Good/CSAP0001/`
- Violation specimen: `specimens/Compat.Bad/CSAP0001/`
- Suppression specimen: `specimens/Compat.Bad/CSAP0001/Suppression.cs`
- Fault specimen: `specimens/Faults/CSAP0001/Malformed.cs`

## Suppression

A compiler pragma or `SuppressMessage` is not authorization. Suppression must
match a reviewed policy grant with a finding fingerprint, owner, reason, and
expiry; otherwise CSAP0001 remains evidence.

## Fix safety

No automatic fix. A reviewed, fingerprinted grant must be created in policy.
